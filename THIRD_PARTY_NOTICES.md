# Third-party material

The MIT licence covers GrantDE's original code and documentation. It does not
relicense scholarship providers' names, trademarks, publications or quoted text.
Those remain subject to their respective rights and source terms. Dependencies
retain their own licences.

Scholarship records are editorial summaries and factual research records, with
official source links and retrieval dates. They are drafts, may be incomplete,
and do not establish eligibility or replace a provider's current conditions.

The public source registry contains provenance metadata and checksums, rather
than complete scraped pages or documents. Raw captures are retained only in
ignored local editorial storage and are excluded from Git and website output.
A checksum identifies the original capture; it does not assert that omitted
content is present in the public repository. Review provider terms before
redistributing any additional source material.
