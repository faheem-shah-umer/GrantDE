A clearer starting point for international students finding funding for their studies in Germany.

A directory, built around useful details.

GrantDE brings scholarship information together so you can compare funding, understand the conditions and reach the official provider. Browsing is free and does not require an account.

Sources you can follow

Listings reference official providers, universities or official scholarship directories. Source links and recorded check dates appear on every detail page. Information can change after a check; always read the provider’s current application call.

What a match means

Search and filters compare your choices with the information we have. Known restrictions can narrow the directory. Missing or incomplete criteria remain in the results with a “Needs checking” label. Preferences, exceptions and additional requirements remain visible on the detail page.

Nationality requirements, country groups and country-specific dates are shown on scholarship pages and may require a provider check. A filtered result is not a guarantee of eligibility. Application availability is shown separately from applicant requirements.

Help us keep it useful

A wrong date or unclear requirement can get in the way. If you find an issue, include the scholarship name, the detail that needs changing and an official source.
